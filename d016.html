DAY 16 · LEVEL 1

Have done vs did

~했다 두 가지
📖 오늘의 문법: 현재완료 vs 과거

1핵심 구문

Have you seen ___? — Yes, I saw ___. ~ 봤어요? — 네, ~ 봤어요
Have you seen my glasses? — Yes, I saw them on the table.
Have you seen the news? — Yes, I saw it this morning.
When did you ___? 언제 ~했어요?
When did you arrive?
When did you get married?
I've ___ before. 전에 ~해 본 적 있어요
I've done this before.
I've met him before.
I ___ last ___. 지난 ~에 ~했어요
I went there last year.
I called him last night.
💬 오늘의 표현: It slipped my mind. — 깜빡했어요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • have + p.p.(끝난 모양): 언제인지 말하지 않는 「지금까지」의 일
  • 과거: yesterday, last week, in 2020처럼 끝난 때가 있을 때
  • When으로 묻는 질문은 과거로: When did you come?
  • 처음엔 have + p.p.로 묻고, 자세한 이야기는 과거로 이어 가요.
예특징
지금까지I've been to Busan.언제인지 말 안 함
끝난 때I went to Busan last year.때가 있음
질문Have you met him? → When did you meet him?경험 → 때
I've seen him yesterday. → I saw him yesterday. yesterday처럼 끝난 때가 있으면 과거로 말해요.

4소리 원리

I met him at a wedding in 2019.
🔊 아이 메림 애러 웨딩
— h 약하게 + t 굴리기: met him → 「메림」, at a → 「애러」
When did you start your new job?
🔊 웬 디쥬 스타츄어 뉴 잡
— d + y → 「쥬」, t + y → 「츄」: start your → 「스타츄어」
