DAY 5 · LEVEL 1

What I don't do · Do you…?

안 해요 · ~해요?
📖 오늘의 문법: 안 해요 · ~해요? (do / does)

1핵심 구문

I don't ___. 나는 ~ 안 해요
I don't drink coffee.
I don't know.
He / She doesn't ___. 그는 ~ 안 해요
He doesn't eat pork.
She doesn't drive.
Do you ___? ~해요? / ~ 있어요?
Do you like kimchi?
Do you need help?
Does it ___? 그거 ~해요?
Does it hurt?
Does it have nuts?
What do you ___? 뭘 ~해요?
What do you want?
What do you think?
💬 오늘의 표현: It's up to you. — 당신이 정하세요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 「안 한다」는 don't + 동사: I don't eat meat.
  • he/she/it이면 doesn't + 동사 (동사에 -s를 붙이지 않아요)
  • 물을 때: Do you ~? / Does she ~?
  • 대답: Yes, I do. / No, I don't. / Yes, she does.
누가안 한다~해요?
I / you / we / theydon't workDo you work?
he / she / itdoesn't workDoes she work?
대답Yes, I do. / No, I don't.Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
She doesn't likes coffee. → She doesn't like coffee. doesn't 뒤에는 -s 없는 동사가 와요.

4소리 원리
