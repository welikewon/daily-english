📖 오늘의 문법: 정해 둔 앞일 (be + -ing)

1핵심 구문

What are you doing ___? ~에 뭐 해요? (계획)
What are you doing after church?
What are you doing on Sunday afternoon?
I'm ___ing (tomorrow). ~하기로 했어요
I'm meeting him tomorrow.
I'm working on Saturday.
We're leaving at ___. ~시에 출발해요
We're leaving at seven.
We're leaving at noon.
The ___ starts at ___. ~은 ~시에 시작해요 (일정)
The service starts at eleven.
The movie starts at eight.
💬 오늘의 표현: I'm tied up. — 바빠서 꼼짝 못 해요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 이미 정해 둔 계획은 am/is/are + -ing으로 말해요: I'm meeting him tomorrow.
  • tomorrow, tonight, next week 같은 앞날 말을 함께 써요.
  • 시간표·일정(기차, 예배 시작)은 현재형: The train leaves at 7.
  • 물을 때: What are you doing this Saturday?
예
계획I'm flying to Jeju on Friday.
계획 질문What are you doing tonight?
시간표The bus leaves at 8:30.
시간표 질문What time does the concert start?
I meet my friend tomorrow. (약속) → I'm meeting my friend tomorrow. 이미 잡힌 약속은 am/is/are + -ing이 자연스러워요.
