1핵심 구문

I was ___ing when ___. ~하고 있는데 ~했어요
I was sleeping when you called.
I was driving when it started to snow.
Have you ever ___? ~해 본 적 있어요?
Have you ever been to Seoul?
Have you ever tried tteok?
I've just ___. 방금 ~했어요

I've just arrived.
How long have you ___? ~한 지 얼마나 됐어요?

It was ___ed in ___. ~년에 ~되었어요
It was built in 1990.
It was painted in May.
💬 오늘의 표현: Keep it up! — 계속 그렇게 해요!

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • was/were + -ing: 그때 하고 있던 일
  • have + p.p.(끝난 모양): 지금까지의 일 (ever, never, just, already, yet)
  • How long ~? + for / since
  • 끝난 때(yesterday, ago)가 있으면 과거
  • be + p.p.: ~되다
예
그때 하던 중I was cooking.
지금까지I've cooked bulgogi.
끝난 때I cooked it yesterday.
~되다It was cooked by my wife.
I have visited Busan last year. → I visited Busan last year. last year처럼 끝난 때가 있으면 과거.
