Have you ever…?

🗣 바로 통역 · Live interpreter한국어 ↔ English

1핵심 구문

Have you ever ___? ~해 본 적 있어요?
Have you ever tried kimchi?
Have you ever been to Busan?
I've never ___. 한 번도 ~ 안 해 봤어요
I've never been there.
I've never seen it.
I've been to ___. ~에 가 봤어요
I've been to Vietnam.
I've been to New York.
How many times have you ___? 몇 번 ~해 봤어요?
How many times have you been there?
How many times have you read it?
💬 오늘의 표현: once in a lifetime — 평생 한 번뿐인

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 「~해 본 적 있다」는 have + p.p.(끝난 모양)
  • Have you ever ~? = ~해 본 적 있어요?
  • never = 한 번도 ~ 안 해 봤다
  • 「가 본 적 있다」는 gone이 아니라 been: I've been to Jeju.
예
질문Have you ever eaten kimchi?
있다I've eaten it twice.
없다I've never eaten it.
가 봤다I've been to Japan.
Did you ever been to Korea? → Have you ever been to Korea? 경험을 물을 때는 have + p.p.
