DAY 11 · LEVEL 1

I was doing…

~하고 있었어요
📖 오늘의 문법: ~하던 중이었어요 (과거 진행)

1핵심 구문

I was ___ing when ___. ~하고 있는데 ~했어요
I was cooking when you called.
I was driving when it started to rain.
What were you ___ing? 뭘 ~하고 있었어요?
What were you doing?
What were you watching?
I wasn't ___ing. ~하고 있지 않았어요
I wasn't listening.
I wasn't sleeping.
We were ___ing at ___. ~시에 ~하고 있었어요
We were eating at seven.
We were praying at midnight.
💬 오늘의 표현: out of the blue — 갑자기, 뜬금없이

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 「그때 ~하고 있었다」는 was/were + 동사-ing.
  • I was driving. / They were eating.
  • 하던 중에 무슨 일이 생기면: I was driving when you called.
  • 길게 하던 일(was -ing)에 짧은 일(과거)이 끼어들어요.
누가~하고 있었다~하고 있지 않았다
I / he / she / itwas workingwasn't working
we / you / theywere workingweren't working
질문Was he working?What were you doing?
I was call you. → I was calling you. / I called you. was 뒤에는 -ing, 아니면 그냥 과거(called)로.

4소리 원리

What were you doing?
🔊 와러워유 두잉
— t 굴리기 + 약하게: What were → 「와러워」
I was driving, so I didn't answer.
🔊 아이워즈 드라이빙, 쏘 아이 디든 앤써
— 약하게: was → 「워즈/으즈」 (힘을 빼고 짧게)
