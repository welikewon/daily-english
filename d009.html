어제 한 일
📖 오늘의 문법: 어제 한 일 (과거 시제)

1핵심 구문

I ___ed yesterday. 어제 ~했어요
I worked late yesterday.
I called my mom yesterday.
I went to ___. ~에 갔어요
I went to the market.
I went to bed early.
I didn't ___. ~ 안 했어요
I didn't sleep well.
I didn't see it.
Did you ___? ~했어요?
Did you eat?
Did you sleep well?
What did you ___? 뭘 ~했어요?
What did you do?
What did you buy?
💬 오늘의 표현: I slept like a log. — 세상 모르고 잤어요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 지난 일은 동사 + -ed: work → worked, pray → prayed
  • 모양이 바뀌는 동사도 있어요: go → went, eat → ate, see → saw, have → had
  • 안 했다: didn't + 동사 원래 모양
  • 물을 때: Did you ~? 대답: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
예예
-edwork → workedcall → called
모양이 바뀜go → wenteat → ate
안 했다 / 했어요?I didn't go.Did you go?
Did you went? → Did you go? did가 이미 「과거」를 맡으니 뒤 동사는 원래 모양으로.

4소리 원리

🔊 위 프레이드 퍼 더 씨리 디스 애프터눈
— -ed 소리: prayed → 「드」, walked → 「트」, waited → 「이드」(웨이리드) · city → 「씨리」
Did you have breakfast?
🔊 디쥬 해브 브렉퍼스트 ↗
— d + y → 「쥬」: Did you → 「디쥬」
