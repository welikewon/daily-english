2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 정해 둔 계획: I'm meeting ~ / 마음먹은 일: I'm going to ~
  • 그 자리에서 정함·도움: I'll ~ / 짐작: might
  • 능력·부탁: can, could / 규칙: must, mustn't
  • 조언: should / 사정: have to / 원함: would like
  • 시킬 때: 동사부터, 같이 하자: Let's ~
말하고 싶은 것예
계획We're leaving at six.
돕겠다I'll carry it.
아마It might rain.
부탁Could you open it?
조언You should rest.
같이 하자Let's go.
I will to help you. / You should to rest. → I'll help you. / You should rest. will, should, can, might, must 뒤에는 to 없이 동사.
