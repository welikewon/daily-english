DAY 13 · LEVEL 1

Just / already / yet

방금 · 벌써 · 아직
📖 오늘의 문법: just · already · yet

1핵심 구문

I've just ___. 방금 ~했어요
I've just arrived.
I've just eaten.
I've already ___. 벌써 ~했어요
I've already paid.
I've already told him.
Have you ___ yet? (이제) ~했어요?
Have you called yet?
Have you packed yet?
I haven't ___ yet. 아직 ~ 안 했어요
I haven't decided yet.
I haven't eaten yet.
💬 오늘의 표현: I'm all set. — 준비 다 됐어요 / 필요한 거 없어요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • just = 방금: I've just eaten.
  • already = 벌써, 이미: I've already paid.
  • yet = 질문에서 「(이제) ~했어요?」, 부정에서 「아직」
  • just·already는 have와 동사 사이에, yet은 문장 끝에.
말예뜻
justI've just arrived.방금
alreadyWe've already finished.벌써
yet (질문)Has she called yet?(이제) ~했어요?
yet (부정)She hasn't called yet.아직
I have yet eaten. → I haven't eaten yet. yet은 부정·질문에서, 문장 끝에 써요.

4소리 원리

I've just made some coffee.
🔊 아이브 저스메이드 썸 커피
— t 빠짐: just made → 「저스메이드」 · just에 힘을 줘요
Have you finished your homework yet?
🔊 해뷰 피니쉬츄어 홈웍 옛
— t + y → 「츄」: finished your → 「피니쉬츄어」
