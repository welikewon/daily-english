1핵심 구문

I have ___. ~이 있어요
I have two kids.
I have a cold.
Do you have ___? ~ 있어요?
Do you have a charger?
Do you have time?
I've got ___. ~이 있어요 (말할 때)
I've got a meeting.
I've got a headache.
We don't have ___. ~이 없어요
We don't have milk.
We don't have time.
💬 오늘의 표현: my treat — 제가 낼게요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 「가지고 있다, ~이 있다」는 have: I have a car.
  • he/she/it이면 has: She has a cold.
  • 말할 때는 have got도 같은 뜻으로 써요: I've got a cold.
  • 없다: don't have / 있어요?: Do you have ~?
  • 미국 영어에서는 Do you have ~?가 훨씬 많이 쓰여요.
누가있다같은 뜻
I / you / we / theyhaveI've got
he / she / ithasShe's got
질문Do you have ~?Have you got ~?
I have 30 years old. → I'm 30 years old. 나이는 have가 아니라 am/is/are로 말해요.

✍쓰기 확인

책에 먼저 쓰고 눌러서 확인하세요.
1. 빈칸에 have / has 중 알맞은 말을 쓰세요. My mother ___ a bad cold.
눌러서 정답
has
2. 틀린 곳을 고치세요. I have 50 years old.
눌러서 정답
I'm 50 years old. (I am 50 years old. / I'm fifty years old. / I'm 50. / I am fifty years old. / I am 50.)
3. 영어로 쓰세요. 휴대폰 충전기 있어요?
눌러서 정답
Do you have a phone charger? (Have you got a phone charger?)
4. 영어 순서로 놓으세요. [two beds] [has] [Our guest room]
눌러서 정답
Our guest room has two beds.

눌러서 정답
