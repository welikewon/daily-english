DAY 15 · LEVEL 1

How long · for · since

얼마나 오래 · 동안 · 이후로
📖 오늘의 문법: 얼마나 오래 (for · since · ago)

1핵심 구문

How long have you ___? ~한 지 얼마나 됐어요?
How long have you lived here?
How long have you known Pastor Lee?
I've ___ for ___. ~ 동안 ~해 왔어요
I've worked here for ten years.
I've known my wife for thirty years.
I've ___ since ___. ~부터 ~해 왔어요
I've lived here since 2015.
I've been sick since Monday.
___ ago ~ 전에
I came here two years ago.
She called ten minutes ago.
💬 오늘의 표현: It's been ages. — 정말 오랜만이에요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 지금까지 얼마나 오래: How long have you lived here?
  • for + 기간: for ten years (10년 동안)
  • since + 시작한 때: since 2015 (2015년부터)
  • ago = 지금부터 ~ 전에: two years ago (과거형과 함께)
말예
forI've lived here for ten years.
sinceI've lived here since 2015.
agoI moved here ten years ago.
질문How long have you lived here?
I live here for ten years. → I've lived here for ten years. 예전부터 지금까지 이어지는 일은 have + p.p.

4소리 원리

How long have you lived in this town?
🔊 하울롱 해뷰 리브딘 디스 타운
— 이어 읽기: lived in → 「리브딘」 · have you → 「해뷰」
I've known my best friend for thirty years.
🔊 아이브 노운 … 퍼 써리 이얼즈
— 약하게: for → 「퍼」 · th: thirty → 혀끝을 이 사이에
