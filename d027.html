1핵심 구문

You should ___. ~하는 게 좋겠어요
You should rest.
You should see a doctor.
Should I ___? 제가 ~할까요? / ~해야 할까요?
Should I call her?
Should I bring a gift?
I have to ___. ~해야 해요 (사정상)
I have to go now.
I have to work late.
Do you have to ___? ~해야 해요?
Do you have to go?
Do you have to work tomorrow?
I don't have to ___. ~ 안 해도 돼요
I don't have to work tomorrow.
I don't have to cook tonight.
💬 오늘의 표현: under the weather — 몸이 좀 안 좋은

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 조언(~하는 게 좋겠다): should + 동사
  • I think you should ~ 로 부드럽게 말해요.
  • 해야 하는 사정이 있을 때: have to / has to (지난 일은 had to)
  • 안 해도 된다: don't have to
  • 묻기: Do you have to ~? / Should I ~?
예
조언You should get some sleep.
하지 않는 게 좋다 (부정 조언)You shouldn't drink so much coffee.
사정상 해야I have to pick up my son.
안 해도 됨You don't have to come.
지난 일I had to wait an hour.
He have to work today. → He has to work today. he, she 뒤에는 has to.
