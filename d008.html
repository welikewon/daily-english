It was…

~였어요
📖 오늘의 문법: ~였어요 (was / were)

1핵심 구문

I was ___. 나는 ~했어요/~에 있었어요
I was tired.
I was at home.
It was ___. 그건 ~했어요
It was great.
It was cold.
Where were you ___? ~에 어디 있었어요?
Where were you this morning?
Where were you on Sunday afternoon?
Was it ___? 그거 ~했어요?
Was it fun?
Was it expensive?
We weren't ___. 우리는 ~하지 않았어요
We weren't late.
We weren't hungry.
💬 오늘의 표현: It was a blast. — 정말 신났어요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 지난 일의 am/is → was, are → were
  • 아니었다: wasn't, weren't
  • 물을 때: Was it ~? / Were you ~?
  • yesterday(어제), last night(어젯밤), last week(지난주)와 자주 써요.
누가~였다~가 아니었다
I / he / she / itwaswasn't
we / you / theywereweren't
질문Was it ~?Were you ~?
I was go to church yesterday. → I went to church yesterday. was는 「가다」 같은 동사 앞에 붙이지 않아요.

4소리 원리

I was so busy last night.
🔊 아이 워즈 쏘 비지 래스나잇
— t 빠짐: last night → 「래스나잇」 (자음 사이 t가 빠져요)
The restaurant was full, so we waited.
🔊 레스터런 워즈 풀, 쏘 위 웨이릳
— t 굴리기: waited → 「웨이릳」

5어순 훈련

뼈대
The trip was great.
+꼬리 1
The trip to Jeju was great.
+꼬리 2
The trip to Jeju last spring was great.
+꼬리 3

잇기 but
