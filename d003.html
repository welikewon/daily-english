What I'm doing now

지금 하는 중이에요
📖 오늘의 문법: 지금 하는 중 (be + -ing)

1핵심 구문

I'm ___ing. 나는 ~하고 있어요
I'm cooking.
I'm driving.
What are you ___ing? 뭘 ~하고 있어요?
What are you doing?
What are you eating?
Are you ___ing? ~하고 있어요?
Are you coming?
Are you waiting?
It's ___ing. (날씨가) ~하고 있어요
It's raining.
It's snowing.
We're ___ing now. 우리는 지금 ~하는 중이에요
We're leaving now.
We're praying now.
💬 오늘의 표현: hang in there — 조금만 더 힘내요

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 「지금 ~하고 있다」는 am/is/are + 동사-ing.
  • I'm eating. = 나는 먹고 있다
  • 아니다: I'm not eating. / 물을 때: Are you eating?
  • 무엇을 하는지 물을 때: What are you doing?
~하고 있다~하고 있지 않다~하고 있어요?
I'm working.I'm not working.Are you working?
He's driving.He isn't driving.Is he driving?
They're eating.They aren't eating.Are they eating?
I eating now. → I'm eating now. -ing 앞에 am/is/are를 꼭 넣어요.
