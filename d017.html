It is done (passive)

~되었어요 (수동)
📖 함께 보기: Basic Grammar in Use Unit 21, 22

1핵심 구문

It's made in ___. ~에서 만든 거예요
It's made in Korea.
It's made in Vietnam.
It was built in ___. ~년에 지어졌어요
It was built in 1990.
It was built last year.
Is ___ included? ~ 포함돼 있어요?
Is breakfast included?
Is tax included?
It's being ___ed. 지금 ~되는 중이에요
The room is being cleaned.
The car is being repaired.
It has been ___ed. ~됐어요 (지금까지)
The meeting has been canceled.
The flight has been delayed.
💬 오늘의 표현: It's on the house. — 서비스예요 (무료)

2오늘의 문법 (쉽게)

  • 「~되다, ~받다」는 be + p.p.(끝난 모양): It is made. (만들어진다)
  • 누가 했는지보다 무엇이 어떻게 됐는지가 중요할 때 써요.
  • 과거: was/were + p.p. / 지금 되는 중: is being + p.p.
  • 지금까지 ~됐다: has been + p.p.
  • 누가 했는지 말할 때는 by: painted by my son
예
늘It is cleaned every day.
과거It was built in 1990.
지금 되는 중It is being cleaned.
지금까지It has been canceled.
The church built in 1985. → The church was built in 1985. 교회가 스스로 지은 게 아니니 was built.
